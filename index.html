Our Story

The Story Behind Epic Global AgroBiz

Founded in 2020 and based in Ibadan, Oyo State, EpicGlobalAgroBiz is a registered agribusiness enterprise focused on poultry production, poultry processing, and the distribution of fresh agricultural produce. Our operations include the production and supply of broilers, local and imported turkeys, pullets, and day-old chicks, alongside fresh produce such as eggs, tomatoes, bell peppers, habanero peppers, sweet corn, cucumbers, and other seasonal crops.

Today, we serve households, restaurants, hotels, retailers, distributors, and institutional buyers, with a commitment to delivering quality agricultural products and reliable service. At the heart of our work is a bigger goal: contributing to food security, creating opportunities, and supporting the sustainable growth of Nigeria's agricultural value chain.
